# Failure evidence

`qa/traces/<run>-<project>/<test>/` holds, for **every failing test** of each run: `test-failed-*.png` (Playwright
screenshot at failure) and `error-context.md` (accessibility snapshot of the page). Full Playwright traces
(`trace.zip`, open with `npx playwright show-trace <file>`) are kept for the cases cited as defect or harness
evidence: TC-REF-03/04 desktop-1920 (H-01), TC-MTX mdm mobile-390/375 and tablet-1024 and TC-CAP-01 mobile-375 (QA-D-20), TC-OFF-06 baseline (QA-D-19)
and after2 desktop-1440 (QA-D-22), TC-VIS-STATES (QA-D-19). For TC-LST-01 tablet-768 (QA-D-21) only the
screenshot and page snapshot are kept (`after1-tablet-768`, `after2-tablet-768`). Videos and the remaining traces (~236 MB)
were not committed; they are regenerated by `qa/scripts/run-project.sh` (see `qa/reproduction.md`).
